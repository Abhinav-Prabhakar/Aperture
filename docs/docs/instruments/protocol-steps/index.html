Protocol Steps

A protocol is executed in order, under time pressure. Show where you are, what comes next, and what must not go wrong.

Live

Western blot · day 1

2 / 5 complete

  1. Lyse cells

    10 minComplete

    Resuspend the pellet in 200 µL ice-cold RIPA buffer.

  2. Reduce and denature

    5 min at 95 °CComplete

    Add loading dye and heat the sample to 95 °C.

  3. 3

    Load the gel

    15 minActive

    Load 20 µg total protein per lane; include the ladder.

    Caution · Do not overfill the wells; sample will spill into the running buffer.

  4. 4

    Run the gel

    45 min at 120 V

    Run at constant voltage until the dye front reaches the base.

  5. 5

    Transfer to membrane

    60 min at 100 V

    Wet transfer inTowbin buffer with 20% methanol.

    Note · Keep the membrane methanol-wet throughout the transfer.

States

StatusMeaning
PendingNot started. The default for every step.
ActiveHappening now. Exactly one step, or none.
CompleteFinished and recorded.
SkippedDeliberately not done. Record the reason in the description.

Rules

  • A step has one status. Do not derive it from a timer; a timer is not a completion record.
  • Durations are part of the step, not a deadline. “45 min at 120 V” is more useful than “45 min”.
  • A caution describes a way to lose the sample. A note describes something easy to forget. Do not use one for the other.
  • Skipped is a result, not an absence. Say why it was skipped.
  • The progress bar counts completed steps only. Skipped steps are shown but not counted as complete.
