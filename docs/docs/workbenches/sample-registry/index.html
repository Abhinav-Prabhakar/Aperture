Sample Registry

Where the sample is, who has it, what remains, and how it got here. The inventory a freezer lies about, replaced by a record with a custodian and a timestamp on every step.

Open full screenTwelve synthetic samples across four studies.

What is inside

  • One inventory across studies. Sample ID, accession, study, type, status, storage, volume, and receipt date. Quarantined, consumed, and destroyed samples stay in the record instead of vanishing.
  • Storage you can act on. Freezer, rack, box, and position in one mono string, plus the remaining volume and a missing volume rendered as an em dash, never zero.
  • Aliquots and lineage. Each aliquot carries its own location, volume, and state. Derived material points back to its parent sample.
  • Chain of custody. Collected, accessioned, stored, and the latest action — retrieval, quarantine, depletion, destruction, or a volume check — with an actor and a UTC timestamp.
  • Linked runs. A sample opens the run report that consumed it, so the inventory and the result stay connected.
  • Export and print. The current filtered view exports as CSV; printing keeps the inventory and the open record and drops the chrome.

Every state

Offline keeps the cached registry and marks that export stays local. Empty is a lab with no samples, not a failed request.
